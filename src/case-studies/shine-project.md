## Overview

SHine was my first time working in Unreal Engine. I tried to build a Souls-like game in a short period, using the project to explore combat mechanics, enemy behavior, animation states, and atmosphere. There are still bugs to fix, and I would like to revisit the project to improve its combat systems.

## What I worked on

- **Melee combat:** attacks, hit detection, and combat animations.
- **Enemy AI:** player tracking and enemy attack behavior.
- **Animation states:** transitions between movement and combat actions.
- **Day/night cycle:** an adjustable cycle that can be changed in the Unreal Engine editor.
- **Third-person controller:** movement designed for exploration and combat.

## Environment and tools

I modelled the mountain range where the game takes place using Gaea terrain software. The other environment assets came from the Unreal Engine Marketplace. I also worked with materials, lighting, and level design to support the dark-fantasy atmosphere.

## What I learned

The project gave me hands-on experience with Unreal Engine, Blueprints and C++, AI behavior, combat logic, and animation state management. It also showed me which parts of the prototype need more debugging and polish.
