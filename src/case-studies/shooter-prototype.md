## Overview

This first-person shooter prototype was developed as one of my assignments. The focus was on understanding the core FPS gameplay loop and programming logic rather than visual quality.

## Core mechanics

- First-person movement and aiming.
- Shooting and reloading.
- A basic weapon system with hit detection.
- Projectile- or raycast-based shooting experiments.
- Basic target or enemy interaction, including reactions to player damage.

## What I learned

The project gave me practice separating gameplay logic into systems and understanding how player input, weapon behavior, hit detection, and target reactions work together.
